# DAGGERHEART CORE MECHANICS - QUICK REFERENCE

## ACTION ROLL OUTCOMES
- **Success with Hope**: Total >= Difficulty, Hope > Fear → Succeed + gain Hope
- **Success with Fear**: Total >= Difficulty, Fear > Hope → Succeed with cost + GM gains Fear
- **Failure with Hope**: Total < Difficulty, Hope > Fear → Fail minor + gain Hope + GM turn
- **Failure with Fear**: Total < Difficulty, Fear > Hope → Fail major + GM gains Fear + GM turn
- **Critical Success**: Matching dice → Auto-succeed + bonus + gain Hope + clear Stress (counts as "with Hope")

## ROLL PROCEDURE
1. Pick appropriate trait
2. Determine Difficulty
3. Apply modifiers/dice (must declare before rolling)
4. Roll dice pool
5. Resolve outcome

## DAMAGE THRESHOLDS
- **Severe**: Mark 3 HP
- **Major**: Mark 2 HP (at/above Major, below Severe)
- **Minor**: Mark 1 HP (below Major)
- **None**: Mark 0 HP (if reduced to 0 or less)

## RANGES
- **Melee**: Touch, few feet
- **Very Close**: 5-10 feet (2-3" on map)
- **Close**: 10-30 feet (5-6" on map)
- **Far**: 30-100 feet (11-12" on map)
- **Very Far**: 100-300 feet (13+"+ on map)
- **Out of Range**: Can't target

## MOVEMENT
- **With action roll**: Move within Close range free
- **Without action or farther**: Agility Roll to reposition
- **Adversary**: Close range free with action, or Very Far as action

## HOPE USES (Max 6, starts with 2)
- **Help Ally** (1 Hope): Roll advantage die for ally
- **Utilize Experience** (1 Hope): Add Experience modifier
- **Tag Team Roll** (3 Hope): Combine actions with ally
- **Hope Feature** (varies): Activate special abilities

## FEAR (GM, Max 12)
- Gained when PCs roll with Fear
- Spent to make/enhance GM moves, use Fear Features

## CONDITIONS
- **Hidden**: Rolls against have disadvantage
- **Restrained**: Can't move, can act
- **Vulnerable**: Rolls against have advantage
- **Temporary**: Can be cleared by successful action
- **Special**: Requires specific conditions to clear

## ADVANTAGE/DISADVANTAGE
- **Advantage**: +d6 to roll
- **Disadvantage**: -d6 from roll
- Cancel one-for-one, never both simultaneously

## SHORT REST (~1 hour)
- Choose 2 moves: Tend Wounds (1d4+Tier HP), Clear Stress (1d4+Tier), Repair Armor (1d4+Tier slots), Prepare (gain Hope)
- Per-rest abilities refresh
- GM gains 1d4 Fear
- After 3 short rests, next must be long rest

## LONG REST (Several hours)
- Choose 2 moves: Tend All Wounds (clear all HP), Clear All Stress, Repair All Armor, Prepare (gain Hope), Work on Project
- Per-rest and per-long-rest abilities refresh
- GM gains 1d4 + # of PCs Fear, advances long-term countdown

## CRITICAL DAMAGE
- Roll damage normally
- Add maximum possible dice result
- Example: 2d8+1 → 2d8+1+16

## DAMAGE TYPES
- **Physical (phy)**: Mundane weapons, unarmed
- **Magic (mag)**: Spells
- **Resistance**: Halve damage before comparing to thresholds
- **Immunity**: Ignore damage
- **Direct**: Can't reduce by marking Armor Slots

## KEY FORMULAS
- **PC Damage Thresholds**: Level + Armor's thresholds
- **Weapon Damage**: [Proficiency]d[die]+modifier
- **Unarmed Damage**: [Proficiency]d4
- **Spellcast Damage**: [Spellcast trait]d[die]+modifier

## STRESS/HP OVERFLOW
- When last Stress marked → Vulnerable
- When must mark Stress but can't → Mark 1 HP
- When last HP marked → Fall (PC makes death move)

## SPOTLIGHT
- Moves organically or via mechanical trigger
- No rigid initiative or action economy
- Player describes action, spotlight swings to next

## GM MOVE TRIGGERS
- PC rolls with Fear
- PC fails action roll
- PC does something with consequences
- GM has golden opportunity
- PC looks to GM for what happens
